# svlings

> one small broken circuit at a time

**svlings** is our SystemVerilog course. It works the way
[Rustlings](https://github.com/rust-lang/rustlings) works for Rust: every exercise is a tiny
circuit with one piece missing or wrong. You fix it, a tool tells you immediately whether you
got it right, and you move on. No lecture, no slides — just circuits that are almost right,
waiting for you to finish them.

The actual exercises live in the course's own repository, and that's where you'll spend most of
your time — opening files, editing them, running the checker. This section of the grimoire is a
**companion**, not a replacement: read it alongside the course if you want the reasoning behind
the tools spelled out in one place, or if you'd rather come back to a page here than dig through
the repo's own docs folder.

Nothing here assumes you've written a line of code before, or wired up a circuit before. If
either of those is new to you, that's exactly who this was written for.
